Guren

A fast photo viewer for Windows. Flip through photos with the arrow keys, and the spot you zoomed into stays the same on the next one.

Download guren.exe All releases

No installer. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

What it does

No stutter

Photos are decoded in the background. The two photos on each side of the current one are ready before you get there.

Zoom stays

Compare a burst of shots at the same spot. The same area stays in view even when the resolution differs.

Zoom to the cursor

The wheel zooms towards the point under the cursor. Drag to pan, double click for actual size.

Whole folder

Opening a file lines up every photo in its folder in natural order, so img2 comes before img10.

Right way up

EXIF orientation is applied, so portrait shots from a phone show upright.

Formats

JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF and ICO.

Default viewer

The ⋯ menu registers Guren for your account without admin rights and opens Default apps, where you pick it.
